# College Hall | five LOD2 sloped roof volumes, offline only

Use `CollegeHallTrial/` (11×7 / 88×56 m) alone. Whole source SHA-256 `8503af9021ec4d195c8708f6c5c5d921ce47c9222c42a556b4bb9ff057498d85`. Candidate SHA-256 `0c4d27110f5d0bb3b7ab3ff2787670e6f08f711d92c7004fcbe2f7481ca5e3ed`, 16,357,734 bytes. This is not Editor evidence or a pmod.

Only the LOD2 body FBX changes (new SHA-256 `81baa6441b5df76822a5d54db7849ace159f3262fe799adaa302baf1c342be51`). Five rectangular blocks swap to five closed simple slopes based on the current middle tier, retaining the existing center gable, façades and base. Roof supports sink 15 mm into the existing caps to remove coplanar contact and avoid a suspended eave. Existing recesses and silhouette outside the five roofs are not rebuilt. Original 92 untouched triangles retain positions, indices, UV, normals, tangents, binormals and smoothing exactly. Old material hierarchy and routing are unchanged. All other 17 import payloads are byte-identical, including the original base colors, control/mask, normal and emission maps. The five roof top, side and underside UVs reuse the corresponding existing atlas strips, not aerial imagery. Center gable texels unchanged. Roof UVs are noncollapsed and the readback normals match their face plane within 5°. The far mesh remains 152 triangles and 228 consistently paired edges.

Blender 4.3.2 readback: 7×192×152 first-hit samples against LOD1. Mid/far p95 improved from ~10.45 to ~1.00 m on the ends, from 5.64 to 0.95–1.00 m on opposite fronts, and from 5.07 to 0.17 m looking down. End outline IoU ~96.60→97.69%, frontal ~95.26–95.37→97.33–97.74%. Retained surfaces which are first hit in both far versions matched depth exactly in these samples. Three matched Blender textured views and one matched Emissive-channel view were inspected; this does not establish day/night in game. Small turrets and dormer depth still simplify at LOD2.

[Roof restoration consultant](https://www.kmarchitects.com/project/college-hall-west-wing/) reports coated metal on sloped/flat areas and slate on mansards, not a per-face survey for this model; roof color/material assignment stays an approximation. In live placement distinguish the real raised north College Green entrance and the east accessibility ramp per [Penn Facilities](https://facilities.upenn.edu/maps/locations/college-hall). Game orientation, collision, traversal and save/reopen are unverified. Irvine remains first for Editor.
