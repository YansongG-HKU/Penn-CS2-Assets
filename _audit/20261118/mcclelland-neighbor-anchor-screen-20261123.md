# McClelland: adjacent assets cannot be assumed map-aligned

The [Penn Facilities plan](https://facilities.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/pdfs/QuadrangleHouses.pdf) places Morris and Bodine behind McClelland, and [PennAccess](https://facilities.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/pennaccess/PA0490-Quadrangle.pdf) describes the upper route via Morris/Bodine and lower route from the west side of Lower Quad. This establishes orientation, not a match of entrances or levels. [OSM McClelland](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/320602420), [Morris](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/320602418) and [Bodine](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/320602419), version 3 dated 2017, serve as dated plan-shape approximations, not survey or post-renovation vertical evidence.

Blender reread the two pinned original adjacent LFS FBXs plus the current terrace candidate. Under per-footprint minimum-rectangle center/orientation placement and unit scales, old adjacent model horizontal bounding envelopes (geometry above 0.20m, no lot pads) overlap the new segment’s envelope by about 229m² with Morris and 162m² with Bodine, calculated separately and not additive. This is a conservative envelope overlap, not actual mesh intersection area. Mapped McClelland rear edge is around local Y +5m for roughly 42m, not the full Y +9.53m rectangular current model behind the rail, and only a short east-of-axis return is deep. A no-contact screen at these box envelopes would require shifting the trial more than 10m into Lower Quad; do not use that shift as a real fit.

Treat the current FBX as independent only. Next model pass: preserve the Lower Quad facade, shorten the long rear deck to a provisional stepped outline, then check neighbor corners/matching interface on representative meshes. Restore actual joined ground/entries only with same-scene checks; distinguish low-side arcade floor, upper slope/entrance and stepped terrace. No Editor run or binary push in this screen. Do not classify OSM dimensions as final or cut neighbors based on rectangular envelopes alone.
