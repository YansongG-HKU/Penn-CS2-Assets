# Irvine Auditorium: independently checked closed survey envelope

The building was rebuilt as a **standalone, footprint-constrained, closed exterior reference**. It is distinct from the existing import ZIP or separately fitted six side-roof surfaces. It gives a whole-building basis for replacing the rectangular levels and overly simple tower and wings of the earlier far mesh. Do not ship it directly: sampling interpolation still smooths stair steps and large changes in roof level into artificial chamfers and grooves, and the walls are a plan-derived skirt, without modeled doors or facade bays.

## Sources, units, areas

- The [City of Philadelphia's footprint record](https://services.arcgis.com/fLeGjb7u4uXqeF9q/ArcGIS/rest/services/LI_BUILDING_FOOTPRINTS/FeatureServer/0/query?where=bin%3D%271385697%27&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=26918&f=json) has BIN 1385697, object ID 493440, and identifies Irvine Auditorium at 3401–99 Spruce St. Projected to the existing UTM 18N-based roof frame, the span is 50.806×51.249 m with area 1,941.55 m². Only a sub-15 mm consecutive sliver was simplified, footprint area change below 0.001 m². It has separate western, eastern and southern projections rather than a single symmetric rectangle. The 7×7 lot (56×56 m) has at most ~2.60 m per short-side margin and ~2.38 m per long-side margin if exactly centered, not an entrance/function test.
- The [NOAA 2022 point sample](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9848/ept.json) uses the prior expanded exact 205,337 distinct observations and preserved extraction receipts. Its input is NAVD88 GEOID18. The new reference fits 47,327 first returns from four scan IDs at 24.5–77 m within the footprint. Class-1 roof samples are not a dedicated building mask. Local Z=0 is 14.40 m NAVD88, as in prior roof work; the hidden envelope bottom at 13.75 m is synthetic, not a surveyed foundation or accessible path. Ground controls near the building vary: ~14.53 m east, ~14.50 m west, ~14.66 m south, ~14.81 m central north, plus separate exposed northeast and northwest grades. These are not doors.
- Checked [Philadelphia 2015 scene model](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/DataSummary.aspx?dataset=7146), zip SHA ef5520d253abe80d43c6a8cace02eb2388be2a930814958371f743da24c9d9c3. Its sole `Buildings` 3D layer has 859 features. Irvine and Van Pelt both give zero spatially filtered records, whether interpreted with the declared EPSG:2272 or the plausible metric axis reinterpretation. No geometry/elevation taken from that scene.
- External roof shape checks from 2025 PASDA orthophoto and 1926 Penn archive establish object relationships, not current surveyed facade details or plan-to-point translation. Record 76.68 m was one central high return from source 59; the six scans' highest points in that zone range 74.05–76.68 m. Thin spire remains a measured-fit problem: the envelope tops at 73.95 m and does not certify the finial, while public footprint-derived maxima disagree by about 5.39 m. Do not choose a maximum from an arbitrary source.

## Verification and what still fails

Interpolated heights are rendered as grid triangles clipped exactly to the City polygon. The mesh is a single-valued top skin, so local slopes and shelves are a more reliable metric than exact facade breaks and self-occluding roof geometry. Neither resolution is an actual LOD or exported FBX.

| Grid | Triangles | Median / p90 / p95 abs. Z error | Fraction within 0.5 m |
| ---- | --------: | ---------------------------- | -------------------- |
| 0.64 m reference | 10,964 | 0.074 / 1.782 / 4.194 m | 84.0% |
| 0.32 m diagnostic | 40,654 | 0.063 / 1.033 / 4.085 m | 87.8% |

These metrics use the same 18,955 eligible first returns from scan IDs 19/20 **excluded from fitting**, intersecting final triangles at their XY; all match to a triangle. They are error here, not stated survey accuracy. The steep intermediate bands and highest spire still have metre-scale errors. Increasing density did not remove the high-error tail. Therefore use true surface fits to replace grid bands rather than decimating the scan envelope into a finished LOD. Pins: 0.64 m mesh SHA256 4f842b3f094d4a3cfa7b0c118ee803140c035e0a4038120c1815cada5e579095. Reimported in Blender: 5,484 vertices, 16,446 opposite-oriented two-face edges, zero disjoint-face intersections. The separate 0.32 m check was also closed, with 20,329 vertices and 60,981 paired edges. No game texture/placement/lighting tests were run.
