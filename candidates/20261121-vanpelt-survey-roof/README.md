# Van Pelt–Dietrich Library: measured asymmetric roof massing

**Offline candidate only, not a replacement FBX**. The existing 52-triangle topology-repaired LOD2 remains intact. This study changes the shape rather than only joining faces: it replaces the long uniform roof concept with the outline of the unequal east and west wings, scoped levels, and a checked north-side recess. Proposed shell walls and below-grade closure are synthetic; no claim of facade accuracy, material bake, three compatible LODs or a CS2 Editor pass.

## Evidence and corrections

- [Philadelphia public building footprints, BIN 1386251](https://services.arcgis.com/fLeGjb7u4uXqeF9q/arcgis/rest/services/LI_BUILDING_FOOTPRINTS/FeatureServer/0) and [2025 PASDA orthophoto](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/DataSummary.aspx?dataset=7157), cross-checked with [2022 Philadelphia NOAA LiDAR 9848](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/70174). The 60 downloaded/cached EPT nodes totaled 18,044,672 compressed bytes. Local crop 799,510 unique six-field records, horizontal EPSG 6347 and NAVD88 GEOID18 vertical in metres. Two sources, 19/20, withheld for the Z fits; the edge plan is approximate and not an independent XY survey.
- The city footprint with south steps projection is 142.042 × 56.693 m in the dominant building axes. The study body after trimming the projection still occupies 142.042 × 49.779 m. The old 18×6 lot is only 144 × 48 m; both geometries exceed its depth at 1:1. Including the projected step footprint, 18×8 is the minimum arithmetic grid rounding and has under 1 m side clearance if perfectly centered. It does not prove safe entrances, pathing or a legal game lot.
- At the site-verified northern parking/grade pocket, a horizontal roof would be plainly wrong. The central recess control at local X [−16.5, −1.2], Y [16,19] has 133 held first returns (p5/p50/p95 16.45 / 17.95 / 19.126 m); 2025 imagery shows the adjacent exposed access between the two wings. The study removes the above-grade shell there; this does not exclude a subsurface connection, which is not modeled.
- Five geometry elevations applied from limited held height controls: low perimeter **38.34 m**, connected roof **42.03 m**, east main upper deck **44.49 m**, west upper local volume **46.56 m**, higher east rectangle **48.99 m**. Selected height controls' held medians are 0.02–0.04 m and p90 0.04–0.11 m. Unscreened points in the entire patches are a stricter check: middle p50/p90 0.06/1.55 m, east deck 0.43/3.05 m, higher east rectangle 0.02/0.05 m. Broad low perimeter includes large residuals (p90 ~15.86 m on its main patch); likely mixed roof and below-eave returns or unfinished edges, not validated as a whole. Do not report height-control accuracy as building-wide. Remaining HVAC, parapets, terrace breaks and intermediate 44.9–48.3 m details are not modeled; purpose/material of the 48.99 m roof mass is not assumed.
- The baseline z=0 uses the previous exterior control at the foot of the south approach (19.331 m NAVD88); this is not the door sill. [PennAccess](https://facilities.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/pennaccess/PA0580-VanPeltLibrary.pdf) confirms a stair-top main entry and a separate accessible level entry at the southeast; do not use the same approach geometry. South projection was deliberately clipped from this roof body. The model excludes the stairs, canopy, all facade panels, windows and vegetation.
- The generated roof candidate is 509 vertices / 1,014 triangles / 1,521 oppositely wound two-face edges. Its independent Blender import verified no nonadjacent triangle intersections. Existing LOD2 topology repair was re-read as a separate input, no overwrite. Nominal 18×6 deck and camera ground rendering are visual references only.

## Reproduction and next repair

Query the city layer for BIN 1386251 with outSR=26918. Scope the listed EPT nodes and run the crop extractor; preserve the crop bounds and no-truncation lengths in measurements.json. Input measurements and sources are read from the working directory; fit_vanpelt_roofs.py generates deliver/ and the original OBJ SHA. preview_vanpelt.py expects the candidate OBJ, the previously repaired game LOD2 FBX, and an output directory. Use two Blender threads with image search disabled and no denoising. Verify horizontal roof transitions and wall heights against source photography (particularly the low perimeter and unresolved east equipment), then implement both entrances with terrain controls, bake materials and check transitions across all LODs. Keep existing game assets untouched until editor and placement QA.
