# Irvine Auditorium 2022 measured roof controls

Independent cloud result with QGIS 3.40 and official EPT [NOAA 9848](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9848/ept.json), published with [NAVD88 GEOID18 metres / NAD83(2011) UTM 18N](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/laz/geoid18/9848/index.html). No system install, credentials or whole-city cloud fetch. Root EPT hierarchy was spatially pruned to depth 10 and 23 bounded LAZ node payloads (5,648,394 bytes; exact SHA receipts). The zone clipped with four separate queries - each below its own point cap - returned 53,270 distinct ordered observations in projected 44m × 34m. Extraction is bounded by data hierarchy depth and spatial extent, not a claim of all available points; keep dataset-year labels on use. Raw sector points were class 1 and first returns even though the collection classifies terrain; we did not invent a class-6 label. No actual building vertex is edited here.

The 2025 orthophoto has tall-structure parallax: the roof plane sectors had to be recentered on a high-point cluster about 4 m from the apparent orthophoto center. Four directional major slope patches fit, with entire scan source IDs 19/20 held out, not randomly sampled neighbors; each held-out sector has both good plane support and some unrelated/complex surfaces. They leave ~1.5 m residual against a single simple convergence and should not be forced into a single pyramid/apex. The layer includes distinct three flat wing roof strips at median 42.40/42.44/42.46 m NAVD88, with all six recorded sources represented. Multiple independent scan IDs reach central classified points above 74 m NAVD88, with highest retained non-noise central hit 76.68 m. Nearby pavement controls 14.10–14.51 m are from the 2022 bare-earth product; the maximum hit is not a survey-confirmed finial height or an exact sill-to-tip measurement. Set distinct roof tiers, center and approximate elevation controls; match 1926 section landmarks and modern ortho separately. Use additional 3D roof projections and line transitions before any precision remake. Current slate and Editor trial candidates are unchanged; no game acceptance.
