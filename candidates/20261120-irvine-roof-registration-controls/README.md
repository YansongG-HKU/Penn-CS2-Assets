# Irvine Auditorium registration controls, separate from the offline slate fix

This directory records independently digitized controls; no mesh, atlas or canonical model was altered.

* [Original Penn archive section and roof plan, 180.I.A.17 sheet 9](https://www.design.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/180.I.A.17_sheet%209.jpg), SHA-256 587828e66d501ff128ccdfabc1a9338f045d3b46f913fa43e24e0dcef72df80e. Horace Trumbauer work 2586; 1926-08-03 contract stamp and printed 1/8 in = 1 ft. Both raster axes were calibrated against the plan's nominal 53 ft 2 in annotation: ~7.73 and 7.71 px/ft. The explicitly labelled grade reference is on the right. Approximate historical intervals: 10.52 m eave to slope/spire-cladding break; 13.15 m from that break to the finial cross feature. Current model corresponding rough intervals are 11.5 m and 16.0 m; they cannot both be matched by one uniform shift/scale. Picks, tolerances and landmark definitions are in section-scale-control.json; the draft height or finial symbol is not current surveyed height.
* [2025 PASDA published ortho](https://imagery.pasda.psu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/pasda/PhiladelphiaImagery2025/MapServer). Crop request EPSG4326 −75.19353,39.95030,−75.19237,39.95135 to EPSG26918 (layer 3, PNG32, 740×740). Raster SHA-256 bc652cd8e4f0253a4b3ff7a98c22368480e24fcc5d96eed19876516f44cb2cff. Measured apparent central four-sided slate envelope, approx 18.32–18.68 m per side, and distinct lower hipped volumes. It is not the surrounding modeled 35.2 m octagon diameter, and tall-building lean and occluded corners have not been corrected. Store approximate 2D polygon and comparisons, not a final fit.
* [2022 NOAA Philadelphia 3ft bare-earth DEM](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/dem/PA_Phil_DEM_2022_9849/Philadelphia_dem_3ft_2022.tif). EPSG:6565 window -projwin 2685030 235542 2685588 235069; output 186×158 SHA f2cd1739eaee8af85ccd356e8d4f7acbf6da142535dfc5b4e35139275a265c73. Verified survey-foot NAVD88; five local ground pavement medians converted to metres span 14.10–14.51 m. No valid roof or entrance sill elevation from this bare-earth DEM. Never equate image grade, lowest surveyed ground or entrance.
