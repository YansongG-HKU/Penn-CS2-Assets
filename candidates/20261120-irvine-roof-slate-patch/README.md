# Irvine Auditorium LOD2 selective slate atlas correction

This patch requires the exact 16-payload offline IrvineAuditorium trial directory from candidates/20261120-irvine-editor-trial (Penn draft 278ddce43b35c7b67edbb04fd424a3ead57af78b). It is a texture-only validation candidate. All five FBX files, aliases, near textures, emissive texture and other texels are unchanged.

Actual sources inspected: [Penn 1926 longitudinal section, sheet 180.I.A.17-9](https://www.design.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/180.I.A.17_sheet%209.jpg), [Penn southeast entrance photo](https://spaceandevents.universitylife.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Irvine_-_Southeast_Entrance.jpg), and [PASDA 2025 orthophoto service](https://imagery.pasda.psu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/pasda/PhiladelphiaImagery2025/MapServer). Orthophoto crop request: EPSG4326 bounds −75.19353,39.95030,−75.19237,39.95135, output EPSG26918, layer 3 and 740×740. Actual output EPSG26918 bounds 483459.6550,4422258.7422 through 483576.4082,4422375.4953 (~0.158 m/px). Photos corroborate broad slate facets and distinct projecting volumes, not removal of real roof accents. Historical chart scale is not used to rescale model geometry.

Top-down rays against LOD1 and LOD2 resolve each model's visible coordinates, triangles/normals and original UVs. The 16 far central sloping triangles lie between model heights 29 and 40.5 m. Limit repair to unshared atlas texels that lie at least three pixels from a non-target UV chart, bright far versus dark same-position mid, where the mid projection hits a sloping surface within 2 m vertical and above the modeled 29 m eave. This intentionally leaves lower-contrast, edge and ambiguous material untouched. BaseColor uses the paired mid sample. Roughness/mask, tint mask and directional normal derive from confidently classified far material of the same triangle (max donor distance 9.434 texels); raw cross-LOD tangent normals are not copied. Original PNG modes and ancillary supported metadata preserved. Edits limited to 795 atlas coordinates; decoded pixels outside those coordinates confirmed identical. Replaying the exact sparse payload verifies all input SHA-256s and reconstructed image RGBA hashes. No geometry or new roof ornament inferred.

Retested the closed 96-triangle mesh, 144 oriented paired edges, 56 vertices and identical full bounds. Twelve Blender renders (before/after, front/rear/aerial, unlit and test-lit with the normal map) show zero silhouette change. In a 5 cm plan projection of 347,552 sampled roof positions, a diagnostic bright mask (mean RGB >65/255) fell from 11,335 to 4,129 (-63.6%). The measure is a texture audit, not physical reflectance or game shading. The visible mid-to-far roof mass/outline change and separate window rendering still require work; no CS2 game-side validation.

To reconstruct: python replay.py /path/to/original-trial/IrvineAuditorium /path/to/new-output using Pillow. The previous trial archive remains unchanged.
