# Van Pelt: seven additional east roof surfaces

**Offline geometry candidate; not a production asset.** Preserves the pinned 00:30 site shell and all previous road/lot and FBX assets. Adds seven elevated plan areas within the eastern attic only. They are geometric patches, not identified HVAC brands, skylights, accessible terrace surfaces, green roofs, material fills, or complete exterior portraits.

- Seed using [Philadelphia 2022 NOAA LiDAR](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/70174) from the earlier 799,510-point crop and the [2025 PASDA rectified ortho](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/DataSummary.aspx?dataset=7157) context. A 0.50 m occupancy grid segments 46.31–46.88 and 47.91–48.36 m NAVD88 training observations (class 1 first returns), excluding held flight lines 19/20. Filter by minimum occupancy fraction and connected area, then simplify by 0.38 m. Confirm on held data without discarding different heights from this validation. Only patches with unscreened interior p90 <=0.40 m and enough in-band observations are integrated. Three proposed patches with inconsistent held heights are withheld, including both 48 m families. Seven accepted polygons total 127.276 m².
- Two levels of held validation are reported. Across the 1,005 affected points at all heights, including boundary mixtures, absolute-error median/p90 changed from **2.05/2.34 m to 0.05/0.656 m**. Across all 10,845 held east attic points, median **0.42→0.18 m** but p90 **2.976→2.970 m** and p95 stays 3.51 m, so significant unresolved shapes remain. These are same-survey held points, not a claim all roof surfaces or vertical walls are survey-quality.
- New mesh 728 vertices, 1,452 triangles, 2,178 opposite two-face edges, same bounds as the earlier candidate. No nonadjacent triangle intersections in independently imported Blender. New component side surfaces are shaped from fitted plane boundaries, not physically measured. Highlighting in the comparison is a diagnostic overlay, not real material color.
- Full [2025 LAS](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/DataSummary.aspx?dataset=7158) is not downloaded: verified range headers and near-west class-6 roof check agree numerically with 2022 within 0.012 m median after plausible US-ft conversion; source WKT is horizontal-only and east roof is not validated by sampled 2025 points. No inference of observed change, vertical datum, or additional validation in the east.
- Penn [sixth-floor visitor plan](https://www.library.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/2026-11/Van%20Pelt%20-%20Floor%20Plans.pdf#page=7) supplies labels and coarse frame, not enough same-level registered points. Does not justify inferring terrace, an interior Orrery Pavilion, or green roof contours from the visible taller equipment. Existing 18×6 game lot still too shallow; entrances / stairs, low-level overhangs, real materials, three game LODs, and CS2 Editor import remain open.

## Reproduce

Use the adjacent candidates/20261121-vanpelt-survey-roof sources with its charted NOAA crop and official footprint JSON. Run OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2 /usr/bin/python3 integrate_east.py with that candidate's measurements.json unchanged. Exact OBJ and evidence go into deliver/. Then compare_refined.py expects its predecessor OBJ, the new OBJ, and output directory. Independent readback uses Blender with two threads and image search/a GPU not required. The CSV source can be reproduced with the prior crop script; relative frame unchanged.
