# Franklin Field measured straight-seating supplement

2026-11-21. This independent supplement rebuilds north and south lower and upper slope envelopes over X −70…36 m in the same registered EPSG:6347 site coordinates and NAVD88 vertical datum as the earlier east-bowl trial. NOAA 9848 first-return point classes and PASDA 2025 orthophoto; no canonical models changed.

- Portal, stairs, railings, inter-tier structure, press box and end transitions are outside scope. Blank cells are evidence holes, not as-built gaps. Four candidate bands use source-group and space-block combined holdouts. Worst candidate P95 vertical residual per band: north lower 0.301 m, south lower 0.371 m, north upper 0.607 m, south upper 0.262 m.
- Mesh topology/UV and archive extraction verified in Blender 4.3 after FBX reimport. Common retained/enforced-empty rays: 24,957/801. LOD0/1/2: 22,184/11,092/5,546 triangles. Max LOD-to-LOD0 difference < 1 mm; not survey accuracy. Each LOD is an open surface with pinned cutouts and reference-only materials and UVs.
