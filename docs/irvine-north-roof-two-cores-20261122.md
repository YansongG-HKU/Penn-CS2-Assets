# Irvine Auditorium – two surveyed north roof cores, November 22 2026

Draft evidence only. Production FBX, atlas, settings, and branch main are untouched. This Blender prototype fills two previously empty areas of the measured roof study: upper cambered panel 42.84 m² and lower panel 19.695 m² projected. New near/mid/far have 20/12/8 triangles with aligned outline and diagnostic UVs; read-back of seven existing reference meshes preserves exact vertices, indices, and transforms. All 7,770 vertical ray locations hit all LODs, none hits the previous roof prototypes in the tested domains, and max LOD difference is 0.02874 m. Open boundary is intentional; no wall closure, final materials, eaves, or roof protrusions were inferred.

2022 fit and held 19/20 are from [NOAA 9848](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9848/ept.json), then tested against [Philadelphia 2025](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/DataSummary.aspx?dataset=7158) April 18 source groups 11/12 and 22/23. City file native Z datum and unit are undeclared. With 1200/3937 conversion and +0.04605156 m local offset calibrated on four nearby ground windows, seven held windows show -0.03542 to +0.11995 m residual. No fit to roof in the 2025 comparison and no horizontal fit. Selection refers to sampled, bounded surfaces only; the 2025 coverage helped define the boundaries. The 1,710 selected first returns were retained regardless of residual. Re-read Blender mesh P95 ranges 0.0954–0.1679 m for 2025, worst 0.2502 m on the 2022 holdout. Not a whole-building accuracy claim.
