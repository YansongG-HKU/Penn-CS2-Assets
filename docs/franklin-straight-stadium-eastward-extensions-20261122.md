# Franklin Field straight seating: supported eastward extension

Revised Blender/FBX candidate for the four existing straight seating envelopes, not the whole stadium. The extension does not bridge to the east curved bowls, reconstruct rows, railings or access passages, close the undersides, or include final materials. Empty cells mark unmodeled areas, not confirmed physical holes.

- Preserves previous profile coefficients and original mesh region through X=36 m. Same datum as the standalone curved bowls and site plan, X east stadium, Y north stadium, E483786.752953/N4422217.429083 EPSG:6347, Z0=11.857455 m NAVD88 (field reference). No extra scale or shift.
- Tested flight groups 19/20 and 59–62 independently per offered cell against the fixed profiles. At least ten returns per group in the explicit ±0.65 m height band; group vertical absolute residual P95 ≤0.48 m, at least 70% within ±0.26 m. All 81 modeled sample locations within 1.0 m in XYZ of a ±0.26 m support point from each group. Also require a full transverse edge toward the old X=36 m boundary via contiguous retained cells. At X=36–38 m, some returns may overlap the original fit. At X≥38 m, returns furnish new spatial evidence, not a new survey or a blind boundary test.
- 217 of 486 retained cells. Worst retained cell–source pair vertical P95 = 0.3351 m; largest support gap 0.4611 m. New plan area 295.3618 m², with 253.5309 m² at X≥38 m and in-band counts there 2,877 / 8,133. Not comparison against every non-seating return in the rectangles.
- North lower 44.20 m², reaches X=50 m; south lower 15.15 m², X=48 m; north upper 63.81 m² and south upper 172.20 m², both X=54 m. The maximum along-stadium reach is 18 m past X=36 m, not at every width position.
- Three tiers 23,920 / 11,960 / 5,980 triangles, adding 1,736 / 868 / 434. Original triangles and UV corners matched the preceding FBXs bit-for-bit on reimport, and corner normals matched with 1e-5 quantization for old faces entirely up to X≈34 m. Shared indexed edges connecting old and new are opposed and paired (62 / 31 / 31); open envelope topology and expected material slots checked. 26,910 common ray probes, 3,222 unsupported probes; max LOD height difference <0.95 mm. This is interpolation agreement, not survey or game accuracy.
- Reproduce: with Python NumPy/SciPy run screen.py then combine.py. The 38,938 clipped first returns in extension-returns.npy have columns local X, local Y, NAVD88 Z, classification and flight ID. Run Blender 4.3+ background with two threads and --disable-autoexec, then build.py, verify.py and prove_old.py with folder after --. The latter uses saved preceding FBX fingerprints or accepts the previous FBX folder as second argument.
- No final textures, game/editor test, LFS changes, production overwrite or PDX upload.

Sources: [2022 NOAA EPT 9848](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9848/ept.json) and [2025 PASDA orthophoto](https://imagery.pasda.psu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/pasda/PhiladelphiaImagery2025/MapServer), which supplies context, not surveyed height or connection boundaries.
