# Franklin Field east bowl: supported north and south extensions

Actual updated Blender reference and three FBX LODs. The central bow and the separate north/south straight trials are not substituted. This is still two open seating-envelope surfaces, neither actual seat rows/railings nor validated connections to the straight stands.

- Input is the previous east bowl archive (SHA-256 cee70194710bd9d5930a33c1dc4db825eec9fe17aa6b31ae8ecfe79e4b3bd3bc). Independent comparisons to the original exported meshes and supplied stand datum were made after reopening it. Same axes and elevation: EPSG:6347 E483786.752953 N4422217.429083, X stadium east, Y stadium north, local Z0=11.857455 m NAVD88. No refit or translation. Inputs: [NOAA EPT 9848](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9848/ept.json), and [PASDA 2025 orthophoto](https://imagery.pasda.psu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/pasda/PhiladelphiaImagery2025/MapServer) for context. Look up exact frames and points in the archive. Point data include original class 1 first returns and source IDs.
- Considered 80 half-height bands each 1 m along Y. For each, both separate flight groups (19/20 and 59–62) required at least 12 observations in the fixed |dX|<1.15 m screen, P95 vertical-equivalent ≤0.40 m, at least 72% within |dX|<0.54 m, and every 17×31 test position within 0.94 m in XYZ of an in-band point in each group. Footprint setbacks and minimum monotone slope preserved. Only cells with a full supported route through their half-band to |Y|=28 m were kept. This is conditional profile validation, not a blind boundary survey; some of |Y|=28–29 already overlaps the original fitting window. No invented fill on unsupported edges.
- 51 of 80 cells kept, about 390.9 m² horizontal projection across two separate levels, not footprint of the combined stadium. Lower: reaches +35 m north and −31 m south; upper reaches +37 and −38 m. Worst retained band/source P95 0.393 m, worst 3D support gap 0.900 m. The bounds differ with height; not the full wing width. No link is drawn to the existing straight tiers or across the gap between levels.

The 11,271 new placement probes agree across LODs (max lateral delta 14.61 mm), while 6,409 absent probes stay absent. These figures are geometry interpolation, not site accuracy or game validation.
