Fully procedural. OSM way 320602420: PCA 45.6 x 18.8 m (~624 m2); ground-floor arcade lounge (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Building split from The Quadrangle (Fisher Hassenfeld/Ware/Riepe college houses).
Ref photos: Wikimedia Commons (Quad_0.jpg, Quad_1.jpg in _audit/ref). No 3D vegetation.
